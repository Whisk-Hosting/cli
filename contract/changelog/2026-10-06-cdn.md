Version 1. `whisk cdn on`, `whisk cdn off` and `whisk cdn status` serve an app's address and its
CNAME'd domains through a CDN on Team and Business. New codes `CDN_UNAVAILABLE`,
`CDN_KEY_INVALID` and `CDN_IN_USE`; `PLAN_FEATURE` can name the setting `cdn`. The skill says
when the CDN helps and that a public page is cached only as its `Cache-Control` says.

Version 1

`apitypes` covers the rest of the API: billing, numbers, operator, workflows, org settings,
data, storage, logs, traces and the integrations, the operator's money (`Money`), the platform's
history (`History`, `NodeHistory`) and the record of what a business owns (`OrgResources`,
`Resource`, `ConfirmedResource`), each closed set of words a named type listed for
`TypeScript()`. `graph.Step.Kind` is the named type `graph.StepKind`. `Approval` gains `org`,
the slug of the business it belongs to. A `[2]float64` field is written as the tuple
`[number, number]`. Otherwise additive: no field on the wire changed.
